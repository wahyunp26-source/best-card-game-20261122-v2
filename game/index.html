You are the Challenger. The Regent has 45 life and a full court of embers — you have 30 life, a 24-card deck, and your wits. Play cards, read the Regent's announced intent, and take the throne.

⚜ COURT RULE: the second card of one suit in a turn gets +2 — chain a suit to hit harder.

How to play: click a card to play it (or keys 1–7). Embers refill +3 each turn. End Turn (E / Space) lets the Regent answer the intent it announced — shield up before a big hit lands. Win by dropping the Regent to 0 life.

Burn and Thorn stacks hurt at the start of the afflicted side's turn, then fade by 1. Snare weakens the foe's next attack by 3. Shield soaks damage first and lasts until it is spent. Your deck reshuffles when it runs out — play the long game, or go for the throat.
